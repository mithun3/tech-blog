# Homelab Architecture

This section covers how the lab is laid out as a whole: which node does which job, and how traffic moves between them.

How the network itself is wired (bridges, VLANs and segmentation) lives in [Homelab Network Architecture](/pages/networking/homelab-network).

## In This Section

- [Homelab Drawing Set](/pages/homelab-architecture/drawing-set) - the lab as it is built, drawn from its own repo: cables, VLANs and services.
- [Why The Lab Is Built This Way](/pages/homelab-architecture/decisions) - the decisions behind the lab: what I ran before, what I chose and what it costs me.
- [Workload Segregation](/pages/homelab-architecture/workload-segregation) - how services are split between the EPYC backend and the Ryzen frontend so that heavy compute does not starve edge services.
- [Node 1: The EPYC Backend](/pages/homelab-architecture/epyc-backend) - the physical architecture of the backend node: massive PCIe lanes for AI workloads, ECC memory, and bare-metal ZFS storage passthrough.
- [Node 2: The Ryzen Frontend](/pages/homelab-architecture/ryzen-frontend) - the physical architecture of the frontend node: high clock speeds, low latency, and efficient proxying for edge workloads.
