# Why The Lab Is Built This Way

Most of this wiki was written while I was building the lab by hand. One guide, one service, one late evening at a time.

Those guides still work. I have left them exactly as they were.

But the lab did not stay still. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a pile of things I had installed and became something I had to design.

This section is the record of that: what I ran before, what pushed me off it, what I chose instead, and what the choice costs me. The [Homelab Drawing Set](/pages/homelab-architecture/drawing-set) shows the lab as it is today. These pages explain why it looks like that.

## What Changed

| Topic | Then | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two Proxmox nodes | [A cluster with a tie-breaker](/pages/proxmox/basics/cluster-architecture) | Two standalone nodes | [Two Nodes, No Cluster](/pages/homelab-architecture/decisions/standalone-nodes) |
| Reaching the lab from away | [A tunnel or a reverse proxy](/pages/networking/service-exposure) | Tailscale, with no port open | [Tailscale, And No Open Ports](/pages/homelab-architecture/decisions/remote-access) |
| HTTPS inside the lab | [Nginx and Certbot](/pages/networking/service-exposure/nginx-reverse-proxy-lxc) | Caddy with one wildcard certificate | [One Certificate, One Front Door](/pages/homelab-architecture/decisions/one-certificate) |

## How To Read These

Each page is one decision, with its date.

None of them is advice for your lab. They are what fitted mine, with the hardware I already had and the amount of maintenance I was willing to sign up for.

None of them is final either. Where I know what would change my mind, the page says so.
