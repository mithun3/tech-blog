# Welcome to the Wiki

This is a personal knowledge base covering everything I learn, build, and explore as an engineer. Pages are organized into sections — use the sidebar to navigate.

## Sections

- **Homelab Architecture** — the lab as it is built today, drawn from its own repo, and the decisions behind it.
- **Hardware** — PCIe lanes, real builds, single board computers, and the console that still works when the host does not.
- **Networking** — protocols, homelab layout, router behavior, and the pieces that make guest connectivity understandable.
- **Virtualization** — virtual machines, Linux containers, hypervisor tradeoffs, and platform-agnostic operational patterns.
- **Proxmox** — a dedicated Proxmox VE section covering why it is useful, how it is structured, and how to operate it well.
- **Self-Hosted Services** — storage, Kubernetes, search, and the small infrastructure containers that keep the lab tidy.
- **GPU & AI** — GPU compute, local AI infrastructure, model-serving concepts, and the practical foundations underneath them. OpenClaw lives here too: project-specific notes on agents, research workflows, system behavior, and the platform around them.

Browse any section to get started. Each page is self-contained but links to related topics where relevant.
