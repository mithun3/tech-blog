It's hard to find good answers in a sea of short videos and shallow tutorials. Everything is sponsored, everyone seems biased, and the useful details usually get buried.

I started this site to be something different: an implementation plan. A clear path from a quick proof of concept to a solid production setup, written down step by step.

That's not quite how it went.

I began with guides, one service and one late evening at a time. Then the lab kept changing, and the plan changed with it. Every time I thought I knew where this was going, something pushed back, and what came out the other side was different. Usually better.

Somewhere along the way the lab stopped being a pile of things I had installed and became something I had to design. The router, the switch and new guests are now files in git, and every change to them starts with a plan.

So the plan didn't go away. It moved into the lab.

This site became something else: an update on what I have now, how I got from there to here, and how I changed along the way.

The early guides are still here, exactly as I wrote them. They still work, and they show where I started. The decision pages show where I ended up, and why.

No trackers, no ads, no fluff. Just honest notes from someone who is still learning.

Whether you're a developer or just a curious mind, this is where stuff actually gets built.
